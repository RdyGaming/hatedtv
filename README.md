# Hated TV

Free US live TV with a guide, at [hatedgaming.net/hatedtv](https://hatedgaming.net/hatedtv/).

Every two hours a scheduled job builds the channel lists, the program guide and the On Demand catalog as plain files and publishes them. Twice a day it also tests every channel link and hides the ones that don't play. Video streams go straight from each channel to the viewer; nothing is relayed.

- Live channels come from the public iptv-org and Free-TV indexes, limited to the United States.
- Schedules come from Pluto TV and from published guides for Samsung TV Plus, Plex and The Roku Channel.
- On Demand titles open in the free Pluto TV app.
